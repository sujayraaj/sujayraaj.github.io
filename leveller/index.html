Wall Leveller

  1. Pencil a small mark where the first nail goes.
  2. Hold the phone upright, at the height of that mark, pointing straight at the wall.
  3. Tap the mark on screen. The green line is true horizontal through it.
  4. Optional: Set scale with a tape measure on the wall so the app can tick the second nail at your spacing.
